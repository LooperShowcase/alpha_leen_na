• Middle East Tensions: Regional friction has escalated rapidly as Iran warns Gulf and European nations that allowing the U.S. military to use bases on their territory for strikes against Iran could turn them into "legitimate targets". Meanwhile, multiple tankers have reportedly caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz amid trading strikes between U.S. and Iranian forces. • Ukraine: Russia has launched a fresh wave of missile strikes targeting power facilities around Kyiv, inflicting heavy damage on Ukraine's electricity network ahead of trilateral talks scheduled in Abu Dhabi. • Poland: Authorities have launched a terrorism investigation after a gunman opened fire in a busy Kraków market square, leaving six people dead and over a dozen injured.

Gaza & Israel. Both Hamas and IDF officials confirmed that an Israeli airstrike killed Samir Abu Younis, commander of Hamas's Rafah Brigade. Meanwhile, 17 countries—including Pakistan—issued a joint statement condemning an earlier Israeli strike on Nasser Hospital in Khan Yunis and urging the lifting of humanitarian aid restrictions as winter sets in. Domestically, coalition talks in Israel are putting pressure on Arab party leadership over Hamas amid ongoing political friction.

Peace Negotiations & Donbas Proposal. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy stated that the U.S. floated the idea of Ukraine ceding parts of the Donbas region in exchange for NATO membership. Separately, Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk announced that an "E5" group—comprising Poland, France, Germany, the UK, and Italy—alongside EU institutions will represent Europe in upcoming peace negotiations with the U.S., Ukraine, and Russia.

• Three-Way Summit in China. The White House announced that U.S. President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping, and Russian President Vladimir Putin plan to hold an unprecedented three-way meeting next week on the sidelines of the APEC summit in China to discuss nuclear arms and the conflict with Iran. • Asia-Pacific Security. The Philippines and Australia signed a new defense pact aimed at countering regional security concerns regarding China. In Manila, a Philippine court ordered the arrest of a jailed, U.S.-wanted celebrity preacher ahead of President Trump's scheduled visit next week. • Peru & South Sudan. Pope Leo XIV returned to Peru—where he lived and served for two decades—drawing massive crowds in Lima and speaking on community suffering during a visit to the Andes. In Africa, South Sudan's election commission postponed the country's first-ever national elections from December to February 11.
