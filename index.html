USA

The United States of America (USA) is a federal constitutional republic located primarily in North America. Key Facts • Capital: Washington, D.C. • Population: Over 340 million people • Number of States: 50 states plus Washington, D.C. and several territories • Official Currency: U.S. Dollar (USD / $) • Language: English (no official federal language) • Borders: Canada to the north, Mexico and the Gulf of Mexico to the south, the Atlantic Ocean to the east, and the Pacific Ocean to the west

LONDON
